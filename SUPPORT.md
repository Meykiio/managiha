## Asking a question

Open a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/Meykiio/managiha/issues) and use a clear title.
Include:

- What you are trying to do
- What happened, and what you expected instead
- The exact error message, if there is one — verbatim, not paraphrased
- Your operating system and Node version

A question that includes the real error text gets answered. "It doesn't work"
cannot be.

## Reporting a bug

Same place, same details, plus the steps to reproduce it. If you can say which
screen and which button, that is usually enough.

## Reporting a security problem

**Not** in a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What is not supported

- **Fiscal or accounting use.** Managiha is a back-office stock and credit tool,
  not a point of sale, and it deliberately does not produce fiscal documents. It
  will not help you satisfy the 2027 inaltérabilité obligation, and it does not
  try to. See [docs/PROJECT.md](docs/PROJECT.md).
- **Feature requests for the out-of-scope list** in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
  They are declined on principle, not on effort.
- **Custom development or paid support.** There is none.
